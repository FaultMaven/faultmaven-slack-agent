# FaultMaven Slack App — Privacy Policy

The privacy policy for the FaultMaven Slack app is published on FaultMaven's own
domain and that copy is authoritative:

**[www.faultmaven.ai/privacy/slack](https://www.faultmaven.ai/privacy/slack)**

That URL is what the Slack Marketplace listing points at
(`app_directory.privacy_policy_url` in [manifest.json](manifest.json)), and it is
where the policy is maintained — in
[`faultmaven-website`](https://github.com/FaultMaven/faultmaven-website), at
`src/app/privacy/slack/page.tsx`.

This file used to carry a second copy of the policy text. It no longer does. A
legal document with two homes drifts, and the Slack Marketplace requires the
listing URL to be on a domain FaultMaven owns — so the website copy is the only
one.
